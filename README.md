# Aurora 🚀🌌

**Dream → Expression → Reality**

Aurora is an AI-guided creator and idea incubation ecosystem designed to help people transform thoughts, dreams, and possibilities into real-world outcomes. Aurora combines creativity, technology, collaboration, and intelligent guidance into one connected experience.

The goal is simple: Take the spark of an idea and help it become reality.

---

## The Aurora Journey

Every creation begins somewhere—a dream, a question, a problem, a vision. Aurora guides users through:

* **Dream**: The starting point. Capture ideas, goals, concepts, and possibilities.
* **Expression**: Turn thoughts into clarity. Organize ideas, explore options, and discover direction.
* **Reality**: Transform ideas into action. Connect users with the tools, resources, people, and systems needed to build.

---

## The Dream Engine 🌱

The internal framework powering Aurora's guidance system:

* **Seed (Raw Input)**: Ideas, thoughts, goals, challenges, and inspiration.
* **Water (Clarity)**: Questions, reflection, organization, and discovery.
* **Sunlight (Momentum)**: Encouragement, confidence building, progress tracking, and motivation.
* **Soil (Structure)**: Tools, resources, education, connections, and systems.
* **Bloom (Creation)**: Projects launched, businesses built, solutions created, and dreams brought into reality.

---

## Aurora AI Orchestrator 🧠

The central intelligence coordinating the user journey through specialized AI roles:

* **Listener**: Extracts meaning from user input and understands intent.
* **Clarifier**: Organizes thoughts into clear concepts and direction.
* **Strategist**: Helps identify opportunities, paths, and next steps.
* **Builder**: Connects users with tools, resources, workflows, and systems.
* **Motivator**: Maintains momentum through encouragement and progress support.

---

## Core Aurora Ecosystem

* **Snap 2 Fit**: Creative product creation and commerce. Users can add any image, and the system automatically handles pixelation and size adjustment to auto-fit it onto any component—whether it's a shirt, canvas, poster, mug, keychain, or billboard.
* **Constellation**: The collaboration hub where it all comes together. Once you reach the soil phase of your journey, Constellation connects you with other founders, creators, and people with aligned business backgrounds or similar dreams. It is built for real-world collaboration, uniting the right people to help turn visions into actual enterprises.

## Repository Architecture & Structure

* `app.py`: Main CLI entry point.
* `controller.py`: Command handler bridging user commands to the pipeline.
* `pipeline.py`: Core ingestion, processing, and metadata management logic.
* `operations.py`: Local media transformation modules.
* `utils.py`: System logging and directory management utilities.
* `gui.py`: Local desktop user interface.
* `requirements.txt`: Python package dependencies (`Pillow>=10.0.0`).
